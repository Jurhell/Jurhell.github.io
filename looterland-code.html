Cop AI Behaviors

There are two AI behaviors used for the cops in the game.

A simple Seek behavior is used for the main cop of the game. That causes it to constantly chase the player.

The rest of the cops use a Patrol behavior that causes them to patrol a certain section of the store until they spot the player and give chase. Where they then switch to a Pursue behavior.

The Seek behavior itself is very simple as most the is done by Unity in the NavMeshAgent class. Most of the work done by me pertains to rotating the cop's model and smoothing it's movement.
